Security

Last updated: November 2026

Your financial data is sensitive. This page describes how FinFlow protects it.

Encryption

All traffic to and from FinFlow is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access tokens for any bank, payment or accounting service you connect, and two-factor secrets, are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and are never sent back to the browser or written to logs.

Sign-in and access

Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app is available on every account, and businesses on the Enterprise plan can require single sign-on through their own identity provider. Sessions are held on the server, renewed at every sign-in, and ended everywhere when you change your password; password-reset links are single-use and expire. Repeated sign-in attempts are rate-limited. Roles (Owner, Admin, Accountant, Viewer) control what each person can see and do, business by business, and each accountant's access is limited to the businesses — and, only if you allow it, view-only personal finances — you choose.

Your security activity

Every sign-in, failed sign-in, two-factor change and password change is recorded with the device and IP address, and you can review it in Settings. We email you about important events, such as a sign-in from a device we have not seen before, two-factor being turned off, or your password being changed.

Data separation

Every record belongs to one account and one business, and one business's data is never shown to another. Your personal finances are walled off from your businesses: personal records never appear on a business page, in a business's books or in its audit trail. Each business connects its own bank and services.

Connected services

Linked banks bring transactions into your bank feed, where they change your books only when you match, book or import them. A payment your customer makes through an invoice payment link is recorded on that invoice once the payment provider confirms it. Other connections are read for display, and importing their data is a separate step you approve. Imports are duplicate-safe, so the same transaction is never booked twice.

Audit trail

Changes to your books are recorded in an audit trail showing who made each change — you, a team member, an accountant, FinFlow support or an automatic process — when, and what changed. The database itself refuses to edit or delete it. The only change it ever allows is removing IP addresses and device details 90 days after an account is deleted.

FinFlow staff access

Every FinFlow administrator signs in with their own named account, their own password and required two-factor authentication; a two-factor code works only once. Every administrator action is recorded with who did it, in a log the database will not let anyone edit or delete, and any change to your account also appears in your own audit trail as "FinFlow support".

The application

Our pages run under a strict Content-Security-Policy that blocks inline script, which defends against cross-site scripting. Cookies are HTTP-only and protected against cross-site request forgery. Fonts and scripts are served by FinFlow itself, so loading a page tells no third party you visited; the only outside scripts are Plaid's or Belvo's, loaded when you choose to connect a bank. Website visitors' IP addresses are deleted after 30 days.

Infrastructure

FinFlow runs on managed cloud infrastructure with a managed PostgreSQL database, connected over encrypted links. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue, please tell us through the contact form (choose "Security report") with the steps to reproduce it. Please do not access or change other people's data, do not degrade the Service, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before telling anyone else. We will not take legal action against research done in good faith within these limits, and we reply to every report.
