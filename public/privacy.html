Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information FinFlow collects, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to the FinFlow application and website (the "Service"). In this Policy, "FinFlow", "we" and "us" mean the provider of the Service.

Who is responsible for your data

For your own account information, and for how we run and secure the Service, FinFlow is the data controller. For the business records you keep in FinFlow — including information about your customers, suppliers and employees — you decide what is recorded and why, and we process it on your behalf to provide the Service. Business customers can request our data processing terms by contacting us.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, business details (name, address, tax ID, currency, country, timezone), and password (stored only as a salted one-way hash). If you turn on two-factor authentication, we store the secret it needs, encrypted.
  • Business records you enter or import — invoices, quotes, receipts, expenses, bills, payments, payroll (including employee names and pay), inventory, projects and time, journals, budgets, documents you upload, and similar records. These often contain personal data about your customers, suppliers and employees (for example names and e-mail addresses).
  • Personal finance data — if you use the Personal area: personal transactions, assets and liabilities, goals, investments, net-worth snapshots, and your owner's salary when a payroll run that includes you is marked paid. Personal data is kept separate from your businesses and is never shown on a business page.
  • Bank and connected-provider data — when you link a bank (through Plaid or, in Latin America, Belvo), upload a bank statement file (OFX, QFX or CSV), or connect another service (for example Stripe, QuickBooks, Xero or others you choose), we receive and store the transactions and account details needed to show them and to let you reconcile them. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. You can unlink a connection at any time.
  • Security and sign-in data — each sign-in, failed sign-in, two-factor and password event, with the IP address and the browser / device it came from. We use this to protect your account, show you your recent security activity, and e-mail you about important events such as a sign-in from a new device.
  • Activity history — an audit trail of changes to each business's records (who made the change, when, and what changed), and a separate history of changes to your personal records.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, coarse location derived from IP address, and error reports, to operate, secure and improve the Service.

How we use it

To provide the Service (keeping your books, producing reports and income-tax estimates, sending the invoices, quotes, reminders and receipts you ask us to send to your customers); to process your subscription; to secure accounts and detect misuse; to support you; to improve the Service; and to meet legal obligations.

AI features. When you use the AI assistant, insights or receipt scanning, the relevant data (for example a receipt image, or figures from your books) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the answer. We do not use your data to train AI models, and our provider does not use it to train its models.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Railway (hosting), Supabase (database), Stripe (subscription billing and payment processing), Resend (e-mail delivery), Anthropic (AI features), Sentry (error monitoring) and, where used, Cloudflare (network security).
  • Providers you connect — Plaid, Belvo and any other service you link, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • People you give access to — team members you invite, and any accountant you engage through FinFlow, see the business data you authorise. You can remove their access at any time.
  • Your customers — when you send them an invoice, quote, reminder or receipt.
  • FinFlow support — our staff can access your account only to support you or keep the Service secure; any change they make is recorded and shown on your business's Audit page as "FinFlow support".
  • Authorities — where required by law.

Cookies

We use a strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in, and we record page views (with coarse location derived from IP address) to understand how the Service is used. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Data security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, connection tokens and other secrets are encrypted at rest, and access is restricted by role. See our Security overview. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and turn on two-factor authentication.

How long we keep data

  • Your data is kept while your account is active.
  • You can export all of your data at any time from Settings.
  • When you delete your account, your account, business records, personal finance data and personal history are erased. The audit trail of changes to your businesses' books is kept, because it is a financial record — this includes the sign-in and security events recorded in it (with IP address and device).
  • Backups held by our database provider are overwritten on their normal cycle.
  • You are responsible for keeping your own business records for as long as the law requires you to; please export them before deleting your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. If you are a customer, supplier or employee of a FinFlow user, please contact that business first — it controls the records it keeps about you.

International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, by the providers listed above. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email before they take effect.
