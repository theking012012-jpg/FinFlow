Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Policy explains what information FinFlow collects, why, who it goes to, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It covers the FinFlow application and website (the "Service"). "FinFlow", "we" and "us" mean the provider of the Service. FinFlow is used by businesses and people in many countries, so this Policy is written to meet the strictest of the privacy laws that apply to them, and we give the same rights to everyone.

Who is responsible for your data

Your account, and how we run and secure the Service: FinFlow decides why and how this data is used (we are the "controller").

The records you keep in FinFlow — your books, and the information in them about your customers, suppliers and employees: you decide what is recorded and why, and we process it on your behalf (we are your "processor"). For that data we:

  • use it only to provide the Service to you, on your instructions, and never for our own purposes;
  • keep it confidential, and limit staff access to supporting you or keeping the Service secure — every change our staff make to your account is shown in your audit trail, marked "FinFlow support";
  • use only the service providers listed below, under written terms at least as protective as these, and update this list before adding one;
  • help you answer requests from the people your records are about, and tell you without undue delay if a breach affects your data;
  • delete it when you delete it or close your account (see "How long we keep data"), and let you download all of it at any time.

If you are a customer, supplier or employee of a business that uses FinFlow, please contact that business first about its records; we will help it respond.

What we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and business details (name, address, tax ID, currency, country, timezone). Your password is stored only as a salted one-way hash. If you turn on two-factor authentication, the secret it needs is stored encrypted.
  • Records you enter or import — invoices, quotes, credit notes, bills, receipts, expenses, payments, payroll (including employee names and pay), inventory, projects and time, journals, budgets and documents you upload. These often include personal data about your customers, suppliers and employees.
  • Personal finances — if you use the Personal area: personal transactions, accounts, assets and liabilities, goals, investments and net-worth history, and your own salary when a payroll run that pays you is marked paid. Personal data is walled off from your businesses: it never appears on a business page, and nothing personal is ever written into a business's books.
  • Bank and connected-service data — when you link a bank (through Plaid, or Belvo in Latin America), upload a statement file (OFX, QFX or CSV), or connect another service you choose (for example Stripe, QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books, Square, PayPal, Shopify, Wise or Coinbase), we receive the transactions and account details needed to show them and let you reconcile them. Access tokens are encrypted. You can disconnect at any time.
  • Security and sign-in data — each sign-in, failed sign-in, two-factor and password event, with the IP address and browser / device it came from, so we can protect your account, show you your recent activity, and email you about events such as a sign-in from a new device.
  • Activity history — an audit trail of changes to each business's records (who, when, and what changed), and a separate history of changes to your personal records.
  • Website visits — the pages viewed, the referring page, the browser, and the IP address. We work out an approximate location (country, region, city) from the IP address on our own servers; the IP address is not sent to anyone else for this, and it is removed after 30 days.
  • Messages to us — what you send through the contact form or in-app support, and the email address to reply to.
  • Accountants who join the directory also give us professional details, verification documents, and an identity check carried out by Stripe Identity; we receive the result, not a copy of the ID.

How we use it, and our legal grounds

  • To provide the Service you signed up for — keeping your books, producing reports and income-tax estimates, and sending the invoices, quotes, reminders and receipts you ask us to send (contract).
  • To bill your subscription (contract) and keep the financial records the law requires of us (legal obligation).
  • To keep accounts and the Service secure, detect misuse and fix faults (our legitimate interest in a safe, working service — and yours).
  • To answer you when you contact us (contract, or legitimate interest if you are not a customer).
  • To understand and improve the Service from website visits and usage (legitimate interest). You can object at any time.
  • Emails. Service notices about your account, security, billing, the end of a plan or changes to these documents always go to you (contract). Occasionally we email account holders news about FinFlow itself (legitimate interest); every such email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect at once. We never send other companies' marketing, and never sell or rent your address.

AI features. When you use the AI assistant, insights or receipt scanning, the data needed for that answer (for example a receipt image, or figures from your books) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce it. Neither we nor Anthropic use your data to train AI models. AI answers are information only and you always confirm before anything is recorded.

We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who we share it with

Service providers that process data for us under contract, only to provide the Service:

  • Railway — application hosting
  • Supabase — database hosting
  • Stripe — subscription billing, invoice payment links, and identity checks for accountants
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Anthropic — AI features
  • Sentry — error monitoring

To show exchange rates and the prices of investments you track, our servers ask market-data services (ExchangeRate-API, Finnhub and CoinGecko) for prices by currency or ticker symbol. Nothing about you is sent to them.

Also:

  • Services you connect — Plaid, Belvo and any other service you link, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • People you give access to — team members you invite and accountants you work with see only the businesses (and, if you turn it on, the personal finances) you allow. You can change or remove their access at any time.
  • Your customers — when you send them an invoice, quote, reminder or receipt, or a link to view and pay it.
  • Authorities — only where the law requires it. Unless the law prevents it, we tell you first.
  • A successor — if the Service is transferred, your data goes with it under this Policy, and we tell you beforehand.

Cookies and similar technology

We use one strictly necessary cookie, to keep you signed in. Your browser's local storage keeps a few interface settings on your device (for example, that you have already seen the welcome tour). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and no analytics company receives your browsing.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit; connection tokens and two-factor secrets are encrypted at rest; access is controlled by role; and the audit trail cannot be edited or deleted. See our Security page. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and turn on two-factor authentication. If a breach affects your personal data, we tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

How long we keep data

  • While your account is open, we keep your data so you can use it. You can download all of it at any time (Settings → Your data → Download my data).
  • When you delete your account, your account, business records, personal finances and personal history are erased. The audit trail of changes to your businesses' books is kept, because it is a financial record. 90 days after deletion, the IP addresses and device details in it are removed; what changed, and when, stays.
  • When a paid plan ends without a new one, your books stay read-only for 90 days, we email you at least 14 days before deletion, and then the account is erased as above.
  • Website visit IP addresses are removed after 30 days.
  • Messages to support are kept while needed to help you and to show us how we are doing; you can ask us to delete them.
  • Backups held by our database provider are overwritten on their normal cycle.
  • You are responsible for keeping your own business records for as long as your local law requires; please download them before deleting your account.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format (most of it you can download yourself, at any time);
  • correct it, or delete it (you can delete your account yourself in Settings);
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including use based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting what was done before;
  • tell you who we share it with and how it is used.

We do not treat you differently for using these rights. Make a request through the contact form (choose "Privacy request"); send it from the email address on your account so we can find your data, and we may ask you to confirm it is you. We answer within 30 days. You can appoint someone to make a request for you. If we decline, we tell you why and how to appeal.

These rights come from, among others: the EU and UK GDPR and the Swiss data protection act; Brazil's LGPD; Canada's PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25; the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and other US state privacy laws; the data protection laws of Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru, Uruguay, Ecuador, Panama, Costa Rica and the Dominican Republic; and in the Caribbean, the data protection acts of Trinidad and Tobago, Jamaica, Barbados and The Bahamas. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live — for example the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, your national authority in the EU, or the ANPD in Brazil — though we would like the chance to put things right first.

International transfers

FinFlow is provided from the United States, and your data is processed there and in other countries where our providers operate. Where the law requires it — for example for data from the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or Brazil — we transfer it under recognised safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum to them, or an adequacy decision, and our providers are bound to protect it to the same standard.

Children

The Service is for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect children's personal data; if you believe a child has given us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. We tell you about material changes in the Service or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: use the contact form, or, if you have an account, Help → Contact support in the app.
