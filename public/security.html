Security

Last updated: October 2026

We know your financial data is sensitive. This page summarises how FinFlow protects it.

Encryption

All traffic to and from FinFlow is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access tokens for any bank, payment or accounting service you connect, and two-factor authentication secrets, are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and are never sent back to the browser or written to logs.

Sign-in and access

Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. Two-factor authentication (authenticator app) is available on every account. Sessions are held on the server and renewed at sign-in; password-reset links are single-use and expire. Repeated sign-in attempts are rate-limited. Roles (Owner, Admin, Accountant, Viewer) control what each person can see and do, per business.

Your security activity

Every sign-in, failed sign-in, two-factor change and password change is recorded with the device and IP address, and you can review your recent activity in Settings. We e-mail you about important events, such as a sign-in from a device we have not seen before, two-factor being turned off, or your password being changed.

Data separation

Every record belongs to its account and business, and one business's data is never shown to another. Your personal finances are kept separate from your businesses: personal records never appear on a business page or in a business's audit trail. Each business connects its own bank and services.

Connected services

Linked banks sync transactions into your bank feed, where they affect your books only when you match, book or import them. A payment your customer makes through an invoice payment link is recorded on that invoice once the payment provider confirms it. Other connections are read for display, and importing their data into your books is a separate step you approve. Imports are duplicate-safe, so the same transaction is never booked twice.

Audit trail

Changes to your books are recorded in an append-only audit trail showing who made each change — you, a team member, an accountant, FinFlow support, or an automatic process — when, and what changed. It cannot be edited or deleted from within the Service.

Infrastructure

FinFlow runs on managed cloud infrastructure with a managed PostgreSQL database. Card payments are handled by PCI-compliant payment providers; we never see or store full card numbers.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue, please email security@finflow.io. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will respond promptly.
