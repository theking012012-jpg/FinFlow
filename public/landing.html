Why did expenses spike last month?
March expenses hit $28,400 — up 34% from Feb. The breakdown:

• Travel: $4,200 (team offsite, approved Apr 2)
• AWS: $2,800 vs $1,100 avg (traffic surge Mar 18)
• Legal: $3,100 one-time (IP filing)

Stripping these out, core operating costs are flat at $18,300. No structural issue — March was just event-heavy.
Should I hire another developer?
At $469K ARR with a 14-month runway and 50.7% margins, you can comfortably absorb a mid-level hire (~$95K). Your MRR grew 8.2% last month — if that holds, you'll be at $550K ARR by Q4. Hiring now positions you to capture that growth. I'd pull the trigger.
