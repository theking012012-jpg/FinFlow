Professional Conduct Policy

Version 1.0 · October 2026 · under legal review — any change is announced and needs your acceptance again

In plain words — for clients

Every accountant listed on FinFlow has agreed to this policy. In short, they must:

  • hold the qualifications and licences they claim, for the places where they work — we check them, and show the date we did;
  • keep your information confidential and use your books only for the work you asked for;
  • tell you about any conflict of interest, and agree fees with you in writing before starting;
  • tell you when they use AI tools on your work, and stay responsible for the result;
  • hand your records back promptly if you end the engagement.

If something goes wrong, use Report a concern on your accountant's card in FinFlow, or email us. We acknowledge every report within 2 business days.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to every accountant, bookkeeper, tax adviser and firm ("you") registered in FinFlow's accountant directory or given access to a FinFlow client's books through FinFlow ("the platform"). You accept it when you register, and again whenever a new version is published. Your acceptance (the version and the time) is recorded.

It sits alongside FinFlow's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and alongside — never instead of — the rules of your own professional body and the law where you practise. Where they ask more of you than this policy does, they apply.

2. Core principles

Based on the five fundamental principles of the IFAC Code of Ethics for Professional Accountants:

  • Integrity — be straightforward and honest in all professional and business relationships.
  • Objectivity — do not let bias, conflict of interest or undue influence override your professional judgement.
  • Professional competence and due care — take on only work you are competent to do, keep your knowledge current, and act diligently.
  • Confidentiality — respect the confidentiality of client information (section 6).
  • Professional behaviour — comply with the law and avoid conduct that discredits the profession or the platform.

3. Qualifications, licences and insurance

  • Everything you state about your qualifications, memberships and experience must be true and current. FinFlow verifies them before your profile goes live and shows clients a Verified badge with the date of the last check.
  • You must hold any licence, registration or practising certificate required in each country or region where you serve clients. FinFlow serves businesses in many countries; a qualification valid in one may not entitle you to practise in another.
  • You must hold professional indemnity insurance appropriate to the work you do, where your professional body or the law requires it, and tell the client if you do not hold it.
  • You must tell FinFlow within 14 days if a membership, licence or insurance lapses, is suspended or is withdrawn, or if a disciplinary finding is made against you. Your badge is paused until it is resolved.

4. Engagements, fees and conflicts

  • Agree the scope of work and your fees with the client in writing before you start, and bill through the platform (section 8).
  • Disclose any conflict of interest — for example acting for a competitor of the client, or receiving a payment linked to a product you recommend — before taking on or continuing the work.
  • Do not take on work you cannot complete competently or on time; if circumstances change, tell the client promptly.

5. Access to client books

  • Use a client's FinFlow access only for the work the client asked for, and only within the access they granted.
  • Changes you make to a client's books are recorded in their audit trail under your name. Do not ask a client to share their login with you; use the accountant access FinFlow provides.
  • When an engagement ends, stop using the access, and hand back or return any records you hold promptly and in a usable form.

6. Confidentiality and data protection

  • Keep all client information confidential, during and after the engagement, unless the client consents or the law requires disclosure.
  • When you open a client's books you handle personal and financial data. Comply with the data-protection law that applies to you and to the client (for example, the GDPR in the EU and UK): keep it secure, use it only for the engagement, do not keep it longer than needed, and report any data breach to the client and to FinFlow without delay.
  • Do not export client data from the platform except as the engagement needs, and store anything you export securely.

7. Use of AI

If you use AI tools — FinFlow's or any other — on a client's work, tell the client, do not put their confidential data into a tool that does not protect it, and review the output yourself. You remain professionally responsible for the work.

8. Keeping work on the platform (non-circumvention)

FinFlow introduces clients to you, and funds that through the commission on fees billed through the platform. Taking that relationship off the platform to avoid the commission is a breach of this policy.

8.1 What is prohibited

For a client you were introduced to or engaged through FinFlow, while that client is connected to you on the platform and for 24 months after the last time they were, you must not:

  • invoice or accept payment from that client for accounting, bookkeeping or tax work outside FinFlow;
  • ask, encourage or offer an incentive (such as a discount) to the client to pay you outside FinFlow, or to leave FinFlow for that purpose;
  • share contact or payment details with the client for that purpose before an engagement is agreed on the platform.

8.2 What is allowed

  • Existing clients. A client you already served before either of you joined FinFlow is not covered. Declare them from your dashboard (or by email) within 30 days of connecting them on FinFlow.
  • Moving a client off the platform openly. You may move a FinFlow-introduced client off the platform at any time by paying a conversion fee: 10% of the fees billed to that client through FinFlow in the 12 months before the move, with a minimum of USD 250 (or the equivalent in your payout currency). Once paid, section 8.1 no longer applies to that client.
  • The client is always free to choose their accountant. This section restricts you, never the client.

8.3 Penalties

BreachWhat happens
First breachA written warning. You pay, within 30 days: the commission that would have applied to the fees billed off the platform (at your tier rate, never less than 10%), plus the conversion fee for that client. Referral payouts for that client stop.
Second breach, or a first-breach payment not made within 30 daysSuspension from the directory for 90 days. Your profile is hidden, you cannot take new clients, and all pending payouts are held. Amounts owed under the first breach remain due.
Third breach, or a serious breach (for example, deliberate concealment or soliciting several clients)Permanent removal from the platform. Unpaid referral payouts are forfeited, and FinFlow may recover any amounts still owed, including by setting them off against payouts.

Before any penalty, FinFlow tells you what it found and gives you 14 days to respond. You may appeal a decision once, by email, within 14 days of it; a different member of the FinFlow team reviews the appeal.

9. Concerns, complaints and enforcement

  • Clients can report a concern from inside FinFlow at any time. FinFlow acknowledges every report within 2 business days, tells you about it (unless that would put the client or an investigation at risk), and asks for your response.
  • Depending on what is found, FinFlow may take no action, give guidance, issue a warning, pause your Verified badge, suspend you or remove you from the platform. Section 8's penalties apply to non-circumvention breaches.
  • Where a breach may also break your professional body's rules or the law, FinFlow may report it to the professional body or the authorities.

10. Changes to this policy

FinFlow may update this policy. You are told before a new version takes effect and asked to accept it at your next sign-in. You have 14 days to accept; after that, your access to client books is paused until you do. Your existing obligations under earlier versions continue.
