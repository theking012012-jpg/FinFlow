How scheduling works
  • Every date here resolves in this entity’s own timezone; amounts total in its own currency; holidays follow its own country. Switch entity above and it all changes — the US books get a US day, the Canadian books a Canadian day.
  • A scheduled document is excluded from every figure until its date arrives (D2). The forecast is a projection, kept separate from booked numbers.
  • A run landing on a weekend or a public holiday in the entity’s country shifts to the next business day.
  • A failed run surfaces in Needs attention; a profile nearing its end shows a runs-left warning; each recurring row links to what it last posted.
Built on F88. The per-entity “today”, the holiday shift, and the forecast horizon all resolve against the entity’s timezone & country — the phase-2 hook this page is designed to sit on. Until F88 lands, day-edge dates fall back to UTC.
