FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free to list my practice? ↓
Yes — completely free. There is no monthly fee, no setup fee, and no charge for being listed in the directory. FinFlow only makes money when you make money — through a tiered service commission (from 10% down to 2% as your client base grows, and 0% on your first three clients) on payments processed through the platform.
How does the service commission work? ↓
When you bill a client through FinFlow, they pay via Stripe. FinFlow takes a tiered service commission — from 10% down to 2% as your client base grows, and 0% on your first three clients — and transfers the remainder directly to your connected bank account via Stripe Connect (less Stripe's own processing fee). You never have to chase payments or do manual splits.
Do I have to use FinFlow to bill clients? ↓
You're not technically forced to, but billing through FinFlow is how you build verified reviews, protect your listing, and maintain your referral commission income. Off-platform payments violate our Terms of Service and can result in suspension from the directory.
When do I get paid my referral commissions? ↓
On the 1st of every month (UTC) FinFlow books your referral pay for each active, paying client in your portfolio and sends what you are owed to your connected Stripe account by transfer, automatically. Stripe then pays it into your bank on your Stripe payout schedule. If your Stripe account is not ready to receive transfers yet, the pay is held and sent as soon as it is. If a client cancels their subscription, their referral pay stops immediately; when they resubscribe, it resumes for the remaining months.
What countries are supported? ↓
FinFlow supports accountants from any country. Payments are processed in USD via Stripe, which supports payouts to most countries globally. We specifically support professional bodies in Trinidad & Tobago, Jamaica, the UK, US, Canada, and Ireland.
How long does verification take? ↓
Every application is reviewed manually by our team — there are no automated approvals. Reviews are usually completed within 1 business day, and you'll receive an email confirmation when your profile goes live.
