Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information FinFlow collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to your use of the FinFlow application and website.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email, business details, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Financial data you enter — invoices, expenses, payroll, and related records you create in the Service.
  • Connected-provider data — when you link a third party (e.g. a bank via Plaid, or Stripe), we receive the data needed to display it, using access tokens that are encrypted at rest.
  • Usage and device data — basic log and analytics data (such as pages viewed and coarse location derived from IP) to operate and secure the Service.

How we use it

We use your information to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Service; to process payments; to communicate with you about your account; and to comply with legal obligations. AI features process your data to generate insights at your request; we do not use your financial data to train third-party models.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with: service providers who help us operate the Service (such as hosting, payment processing, and email delivery) under confidentiality obligations; third-party integrations you explicitly connect; and authorities where required by law. If you engage an accountant through FinFlow, the data you authorise is shared with that accountant.

Data security

Connection tokens and sensitive credentials are encrypted at rest, traffic is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted. See our Security overview for more detail. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage strong, unique passwords.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, plus any period required to meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations. You can request export or deletion of your data.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Email hello@finflow.io.
